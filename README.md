## Projects

### Can I Go?

**Can I Go?** is a travel-planning application that helps users plan trips around their real budget.

The application allows users to:

- Choose a destination and departure city
- Enter travel dates and number of travellers
- Set a realistic travel budget
- Estimate the overall cost of a trip
- See a breakdown of expected travel expenses
- Check whether a trip fits within their budget
- Calculate how much they may need to save to reach a travel goal
- Compare destinations
- Explore ways to make an over-budget trip fit their budget

**Live application:**  
https://canigoapp.online

Can I Go? is the first product developed by **Belvarna Limited**, a technology company focused on building software and digital products that turn complexity into clarity.
