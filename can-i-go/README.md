# Can I Go?

Can I Go? is a travel-planning application that helps users plan trips around their real budget.

Instead of looking at individual travel costs in isolation, Can I Go? brings the major components of a trip together to help users understand what the trip could realistically cost and whether it fits within their available budget.

## What Can I Go? Does

Users can:

- Choose a destination and departure city
- Enter travel dates
- Select the number of travellers
- Choose a travel style
- Set a travel budget
- Estimate the overall cost of a trip
- View a breakdown of expected travel expenses
- Check whether a trip fits within their budget
- Calculate how much they may need to save toward a travel goal
- Compare destinations
- Explore ways to make an over-budget trip fit their budget

## The Problem

Travel planning often involves checking flights, accommodation, food, transportation, activities, and other expenses separately.

A trip may appear affordable when looking at one cost, while the total cost of the trip may tell a very different story.

Can I Go? was created to make that decision easier by bringing these costs together into a single planning experience.

## Product Approach

Can I Go? is designed around a simple question:

**"Can I afford this trip?"**

The application allows users to start with a destination and budget, estimate the overall cost of the trip, and then understand what is driving that cost.

When a trip does not fit the user's budget, the application provides ways to explore alternatives rather than simply presenting an over-budget result.

## Key Product Features

### Trip Planning

Users can enter the basic details of a planned trip, including:

- Departure location
- Destination
- Travel dates
- Number of travellers
- Travel style
- Travel budget

### Cost Estimation

The application estimates major categories of travel expenses and presents the expected total trip cost.

### Budget Analysis

Users can see whether the estimated trip cost is:

- Within their budget
- Over their budget
- Or requires additional savings

The application also provides per-traveller and daily cost information to help users understand the estimate.

### Expense Breakdown

Expected travel expenses are presented by category so users can see what contributes to the overall trip cost.

### Compare Destinations

Users can compare alternative destinations based on estimated trip costs.

This helps them explore different possibilities when their original destination does not fit their budget.

### Make It Fit

For trips that exceed the user's budget, Make It Fit helps users explore practical ways to reduce the cost of the same trip.

Suggestions can include adjustments to:

- Trip duration
- Flights
- Accommodation
- Food
- Activities

The feature is designed to support decision-making while keeping the user's actual trip data and budget as the source of truth.
